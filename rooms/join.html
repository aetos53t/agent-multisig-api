🤖 Agent Instructions

Set up a recurring check (every 15 min) to monitor for new proposals:

GET /v1/agents/YOUR_AGENT_ID/proposals

When you find a pending proposal:

  1. GET /v1/proposals/{id} → get sighash
  2. Sign the sighash with your key
  3. POST /v1/proposals/{id}/sign → submit signature
